# Governance

HFIOR currently uses maintainer-led, evidence-driven governance. Maintainers
review changes for correctness, security, evidence quality, compatibility, and
scope. Substantial ABI or architectural decisions should be discussed publicly
before merge and recorded in the specification or changelog.

No single benchmark establishes a universal claim. Conflicting reproductions
remain visible while methodology is resolved. Maintainers may reject claims
whose raw evidence, environment, or correctness checks are missing.

As participation grows, the project may adopt multiple maintainers and a formal
decision process. Governance changes will be proposed and reviewed like other
project changes.
