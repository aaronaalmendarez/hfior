# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, technically rigorous, and generous with evidence. Welcome people
regardless of experience, identity, background, or affiliation. Critique claims
and code without attacking people. Report negative results honestly and do not
pressure contributors to overstate conclusions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation, doxxing, sexualized attention, or publishing private
information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify, edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens a
safe, constructive project. Report conduct concerns privately through the
maintainer contact configured on the repository. Maintainers will minimize
disclosure and avoid conflicts of interest.

This concise code applies in project spaces and when representing the project.
